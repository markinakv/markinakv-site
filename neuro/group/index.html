Why it works

Neurointegration works on three levels at once. We stabilize your state so plans don’t run into exhaustion. We change the thinking that pulls you back into old loops. We plan actions sized to fit an ordinary week.

StateSleep, energy, stress response, recovery.
ThinkingBeliefs that keep us stuck in preparation, sabotaging plans or grabbing at everything at once.
ActionSmall, consistent steps at sprint pace, plus reflection.

Big goals break against reality. A sprint is four weeks and three projects you can see in full. Short cycles help you regularly notice what to strengthen, and a completed cycle helps the brain lock in the experience of change.

10/9010% of your time goes to the new, 90% of life stays as it is. This rhythm holds up with a full workload, during moves and while travelling.
